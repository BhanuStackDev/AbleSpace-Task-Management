## Before submitting

- Verify the Vercel login page opens.
- Continue as Guest and verify the task workspace loads.
- Create, edit, view and delete a task.
- Verify priority/status changes.
- Verify calendar.
- Verify light/dark theme persistence.
- Verify the backend URL is reachable.
- Keep GitHub repository public.
- Keep Vercel and Render deployments accessible for at least 45 days after submission.
